1. Why are the variables declared as private?
Variables are declared as private to implement encapsulation (data hiding). This protects sensitive data from unauthorized direct access or accidental modification from outside the class.

2. What happens if the variables are declared public?
If the variables are declared public, any external class can directly access and modify them without any validation or control, which can lead to invalid data states (e.g., setting a negative CGPA) and breaks data security.

3. Why do we use getters and setters?
We use getters and setters to provide controlled, safe access to private variables. Setters allow us to validate or restrict data before updating a variable, while getters allow read-only or formatted access to the stored values.
